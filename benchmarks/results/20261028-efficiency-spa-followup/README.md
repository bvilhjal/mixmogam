# Conditional-binomial saddlepoint: paired follow-up

Four alternating fresh-process pairs compare the original source with the
final specialized cumulants, including the safeguards at the center and
support boundaries and stable curvature at extreme tilts. The archived
protocol, source trees, hashes, power state and process resources are
retained here. The synthetic workload evaluates 512 variants at 5,000
samples with covariates and missing calls.

The median paired time ratio was 2.29: median 1.147 versus 0.504 s, with
pair ratios 2.36, 2.28, 2.27 and 2.29. The largest absolute log-p difference
was 7.7e-13, with matching NaN and infinite masks. Median process peak RSS
was 239 versus 211 MiB; individual RSS differences were variable. This is
one component, not a complete GWAS timing or calibration study.

`gwas-parity/` compares complete public HRATT calls from the final frozen
sources in five synthetic configurations with binary and weighted traits,
packed calls and strong-structure refits. All variational fits converged;
the chosen models and iteration counts agreed. The largest absolute
log10 p difference was 1.44e-13, with no decision changes at 0.05 / m and
matching NaN and infinite masks. This is software parity, not a calibration
study.
