# S4 rerun with built-in PCs

The S4 gate rerun of [`20261006-hratt-followups`](../20261006-hratt-followups/README.md)
(feature review 2026-10-08, item 7: *mixmogam: PCs, covariate file, CSV
with N, thin CLI*). Protocol: [`plan.md`](plan.md), unchanged and frozen
before the main run. The runner and the measured mixmogam source snapshot
(working tree at 2026-10-08, before its commit; `source/mixmogam.diff`)
are in `source/`. Run on 8 October 2026 with one thread per process and
two panels at a time (`--scenarios S4`).

**What changed.** The "+PCs" covariates now come from
`mixmogam.pca.principal_components`, the exact SVD of the thinned panel
(2,000 random markers with sample MAF >= 5%), instead of the driver's own
four-iteration randomized subspace iteration. The thinning draws and
seeds are the same as the retired `top_pcs`, so the thinned panels are
unchanged and only the eigensolver moved. Every non-PC case regenerates
byte-identically (same seeds and sampling streams).

**Scope.** The five S4 cells at Fst 0.05 (null quantitative and null
binary 5%, each with `c` and `c+PCs`; mixed quantitative with `c+PCs`),
30 replicates per null cell and 10 per mixed cell: 130 cases, 129
completed. Criteria rows whose cells are outside S4 (F3, F4, most of F6)
are vacuous here and say nothing new. As in the other archives the case
input files are omitted (they regenerate byte-identically from `case.json`
and the seeds); `case.json` keeps the genotype SHA-256 digests.

## Built-in versus randomized PCs (MAF 1-5%, two-sided rate at 1e-3, x nominal)

| Cell (Fst 0.05) | Method | 6 Oct (randomized) | 8 Oct (built-in) | pooled-frequency ablation, 6 Oct / 8 Oct |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| null quantitative, `c+PCs` | hratt-w | 0.85 | 0.82 | 6.54 / 6.64 |
| null binary 5%, `c+PCs` | hratt-bin-w | 0.96 | 0.98 | 3.96 / 3.95 |
| mixed quantitative, `c+PCs` | hratt-w | 0.92 | 0.77 | 7.36 / 7.05 |

The reviewed claim holds unchanged: with PCs the rare-MAF tails sit near
nominal, against 4-7 times nominal when the allele frequencies are
pooled. The exact endpoints move within Monte Carlo error of the
10-replicate mixed cell (its low-MAF count gives an MCSE of roughly
0.13), and non-PC cells match the 6 October aggregates to three decimals
everywhere.

## One failed case

`fst0.05_rep06/null_binary0.05_S4` segfaulted 21 s into its methods
(exit -11). The macOS crash report
(`python3.14-2026-10-08-120658.ips`, *Segmentation fault: 11*) places the
faulting frame in Apple Accelerate's `cblas_dgemm` (`libBLAS.dylib`).
The case was regenerated byte-identically (phenotype, covariate and
weight files and the genotype SHA-256 digests all match) and its worker
then completed cleanly three times in a row, so the inputs and code paths
are sound; the machine was loaded at the time (one-minute average 6.6 at
the case's start) and a macOS memory-pressure event followed five minutes
later. It is recorded as a native-library flake, not as a result: the
cell's pooled rows use its remaining 29 replicates, and every other case
(including the same cell in 29 other replicates) completed.

## What the rerun establishes

Review item 7's gate is met: the built-in PCs reproduce the prespecified
S4 calibration of the benchmark-private ones (F5 passes, the pooled
ablation contrast is unchanged), and with this run `hratt_followups.py`
no longer carries its own PC implementation. Together with the unit
tests (PCs match the SVD of the thinned panel; CLI equals API bitwise)
this closes the ancestry-PC, covariate-file, CSV-with-N and thin-CLI
item.
